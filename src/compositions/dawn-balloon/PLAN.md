# DawnBalloon — 20-second composition plan
1. Balloon travel: envelope and basket move steadily upward (+Y) and forward (+X) throughout all 600 frames, with a gentle side-to-side sway; basket burner glow remains visible.
2. Shot 1 (0–7s): low valley-floor camera moves from [0.15, 1.65, 17.5] to [0.70, 1.65, 16.0], looking toward the climbing balloon as it lifts from just above the valley floor.
3. Shot 1 subject/framing: balloon begins low and centered, rises visibly across the frame without clipping before the transition; near and far ridge layers slide at distinct rates through mist.
4. Shot 2 (7–14s): camera tracks from [5.97, 6.82, 5.19] to [4.95, 8.99, 5.19] around the balloon's center, with a 40-degree orbit; the closer lens distance makes the envelope occupy about one-third of frame width.
5. Shot 2 subject/framing: envelope, four suspension ropes, burner flame, and box basket remain connected and clearly readable while the balloon rises and drifts forward.
6. Shot 3 (14–20s): high camera pulls back and cranes from [5.28, 12.44, 12.8] to [16.38, 22.29, 22.8], moving through and above the cloud layer.
7. Shot 3 subject/framing: balloon continues upward and forward, becoming small against a broad sunrise sky; foreground and midground mountain peaks and cloud banks provide strong parallax.
8. Geometry: deformed faceted sphere envelope with curved peach/cream/rust bands and lower skirt; four taut ropes connect the skirt to a box basket; emissive burner flame; displaced low-poly cones with narrow snow caps.
9. Palette/light: cool blue-violet valley shadows and slate mountains contrasted by a warm gold sun disc, amber directional key, soft blue fill, and depth fog.
10. Sound/timing: procedural 20-second stereo wind bed and low drone with burner hiss swelling in shot 1; Remotion audio fades in for 1s/out for 2s; 18-frame visual dissolves; title “Above the quiet valley” fades within 1.7s.
