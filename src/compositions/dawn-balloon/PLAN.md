# DawnBalloon — 20-second composition plan
1. Balloon travel: envelope and basket move steadily upward (+Y) and forward toward +X through all 600 frames; slow lateral drift is layered on top.
2. Shot 1 (0–7s): low valley-floor camera starts at [0, 2.6, 15], ends [2.8, 8.8, 11]; look upward as the balloon climbs from just above the valley floor past camera height.
3. Shot 1 camera/subject: balloon begins centered-left and large, rises beyond the top third; near and far ridges slide at different rates; mist flows laterally.
4. Shot 2 (7–14s): side-tracking camera starts [-8, 5.8, 12], ends [1, 7.2, 12], with a 40-degree orbit around the balloon; basket, four ropes and stripe bands stay readable.
5. Shot 2 camera/subject: balloon occupies about one-third of frame, rising and drifting forward in the same direction; midground peaks scroll oppositely for parallax.
6. Shot 3 (14–20s): high camera starts [5, 11, 14], ends [12, 19, 24], pulling back and craning upward as the balloon passes through a layered cloud bank.
7. Shot 3 camera/subject: balloon continues rising and drifting forward, becoming small against a broad sunrise sky; mountain tops and clouds move past at distinct depths.
8. Geometry: faceted deformed spherical envelope with three curved color bands, open lower skirt, box basket, four taut ropes and a warm emissive burner; displaced low-poly cone mountains with snow caps.
9. Palette/light: cool blue-violet valley shadows, muted teal and slate mountain faces, peach/amber balloon stripes, pale gold sun disc, warm directional sunrise key, blue sky fill and depth fog.
10. Sound/transitions: procedural stereo 20-second wind/drone bed plus shot-one burner hiss swell; 1s audio fade-in, 2s fade-out; three continuous camera moves joined by 18-frame dissolves; title “Above the quiet valley” fades within the first 1.7s.
