# BlackHole — scene plan
**Format:** 22 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Three.js is selected because the subject depends on spatial parallax, an oblique disk plane and changing camera angles. The references are stills, not sequential motion evidence.

## Scene 1 — Deep field / scale reveal (0:00–0:06)
- References: frame-02 primary; frame-01 secondary.
- Borrow restrained diffuse galactic dust, sparse stars, an intense central ring and negative space. Ignore the tiny final subject scale and exact galaxy layout.
- Technique: layered 3D particles and emissive black-hole geometry. Slow push-in and lateral drift; three parallax speeds. Cool charcoal/navy with white-cyan centre.
- Transition: exposure-led crossfade to the edge-on view.
- Audio intention: sub-bass gravitational swell and sparse crystalline particles.

## Scene 2 — Edge-on hero (0:06–0:14)
- References: frame-03 primary; frame-01 for upper/lower lensing arcs.
- Borrow the large clean shadow, broad blue disk, intense icy-white/cyan crown and partial lower arc. Ignore watermark, exact pixel geometry and over-dense stars.
- Technique: Three.js annular disk meshes, irregular plasma filaments, emissive lensed rim and star depth layers.
- Camera/subject: measured push-in and restrained orbit; disk layers rotate slowly at slightly different speeds while the shadow remains stable.
- Palette: near-black, deep navy, steel blue, cyan and white. Keep the shadow genuinely dark.
- Transition: soft exposure crossfade motivated by the camera changing angle.
- Audio intention: sustained low harmonic bed, soft plasma hiss and slow stereo movement.

## Scene 3 — Oblique plasma sweep (0:14–0:22)
- References: frame-04 primary; frame-03 for event-horizon clarity.
- Borrow diagonal lower-left to upper-right flow, foreground/background overlap, white-gold cores and smoky amber filaments. Ignore incidental crescent/radial marks and excessive shadow occlusion.
- Technique: tilted 3D disk, layered emissive bands and filament curves.
- Camera/subject: slow dolly and restrained orbit; small filaments move faster than the massive shadow.
- Palette: white-gold, pale gold, amber and copper on dark blue-black.
- Transition: gradual exposure fade with the silhouette still readable.
- Audio intention: low-frequency crescendo, granular plasma texture and long reverb tail.

## Per-reference decisions
- frame-01: borrow iconic central symmetry and upper/lower lensed arcs; ignore dense star field.
- frame-02: borrow establishing scale and restrained galaxy depth; ignore tiny focal size outside opening.
- frame-03: borrow large shadow, cool palette and broad horizontal disk; ignore creator watermark.
- frame-04: borrow diagonal depth, warm turbulence and foreground occlusion; ignore incidental marks and overly clipped highlights.

Every shot includes ongoing camera/subject movement, multi-depth stars/plasma and a short code overlay. Audio remains unimplemented until approved assets are selected.
