# DawnBalloon — review round 3

## Ranked aesthetic problems

1. **Basket and ropes are still not connected to the envelope:** The frame samples show the skirt ring below the envelope, but the basket and four suspension ropes are absent from the opening shots. In the mid/final shots the basket appears far below the balloon, disconnected from the envelope. The rope attachment coordinates are in a different local vertical space from the envelope group. Move the four ropes to the skirt rim and place the basket directly below them.
2. **Shot 1 is still clipped at its end:** Frames 0 and 60 show the envelope above the valley floor, frame 120 cuts into its lower edge, and frame 180 shows only a small portion of the envelope at the bottom edge. Rebalance the upward camera framing so the subject remains readable as it passes the low camera.
3. **Shot 2 remains smaller than the requested one-third frame presence:** The middle shot keeps the balloon readable, but it occupies much less than one-third of the frame width. Move the tracking camera closer while retaining a 40-degree orbit.

## Motion check

- The envelope continues rising (+Y) and drifting forward (+X) across all three shots; no reversal was visible.
- Shot 1: motion is visible at frames 0, 60, and 120, but framing clips the envelope by frame 180.
- Shot 2: the balloon and three mountain layers visibly progress at frames 240, 300, and 360.
- Shot 3: the balloon continues upward and forward at frames 420, 480, 540, and 570 while the foreground/midground peaks move at distinct parallax rates.
- **Verdict:** Motion is sustained, but basket/rope rigging and opening-shot framing need a final correction. Middle-shot scale needs to be brought closer to the requested composition.
