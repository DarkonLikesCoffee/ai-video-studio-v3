# DawnBalloon — review round 2

## Ranked aesthetic problems

1. **Opening shot still hides the basket and clips the balloon:** The lower rig begins behind the valley floor and the envelope reaches the bottom edge by frame 120. Even though the subject now reads as a balloon in the later shots, the opening still lacks a clean silhouette for the basket and suspension ropes. Raise the launch trajectory into view earlier, keep the basket above the horizon, and pull the camera back slightly to preserve the entire rig.
2. **Middle shot needs a more deliberate tracking composition:** The balloon is readable but small relative to the wide frame and the basket/ropes remain understated. Bring the camera closer and let the envelope occupy closer to one-third of the image as requested.
3. **Sunrise could be warmer and less uniform:** The sun disc is pale and the sky remains mostly flat blue. Add a restrained warm horizon gradient/glow and stronger warm key fill on the balloon without blowing out mountain details.

## Motion check

- **Direction:** Balloon continues upward (+Y) and forward (+X) with no direction reversal. This matches the plan.
- **Opening:** The frame 0 balloon touches the valley floor, frame 60 has lifted slightly, and frame 120 reaches/clips the bottom edge; subject motion is present but the upward framing is poor.
- **Middle:** Frames 240 and 300 show balloon progression, while mountain layers slide past; motion is visibly present.
- **Final:** Frames 420, 480, 540, and 570 keep the balloon small but readable with clear foreground/midground parallax as peaks pass. The final shot is materially clearer than round 1.
- **Verdict:** Motion is sustained across shots, but shot 1 violates readable framing. Keep the full balloon rig in view in all opening samples.
