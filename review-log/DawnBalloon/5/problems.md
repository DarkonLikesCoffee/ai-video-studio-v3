# DawnBalloon — review round 5

## Ranked aesthetic problems

1. **Sunrise sky could feel warmer and less uniform:** The balloon, ropes, burner, basket and valley composition are now clearly readable, but the sky stays a fairly even cool blue and the sun disc reads pale. A subtle peach/gold horizon gradient or a warmer low-angle key light would give dawn more emotional warmth without sacrificing silhouette contrast.
2. **Shot 1 is more distant than the tracking shot:** The opening reads as a whole balloon lifting above the valley, but the balloon occupies a smaller portion of the frame. A slightly lower camera or gentle push-in could make the lift-off feel more immediate while keeping the full rig visible.
3. **Mountain silhouettes are extremely geometric and uniform in some layers:** The faceted peaks provide clear parallax, but the regular repeating ridge spacing and bright snow tips make the skyline slightly pattern-like. Vary the peak spacing/height and soften a few distant snow caps.

## Motion check
